Need help with Ringvault? Email chandan@mopplications.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Caller ID and blocking aren't working

Open the Settings app › Phone › Call Blocking & Identification and turn on Ringvault. In Ringvault, Settings › Open Settings takes you straight to that page. Then tap Update Extension in Ringvault › Settings. iOS can take a short while to apply changes.

How do I block a number?

Open Ringvault › Settings › Blocked Numbers and tap +. Choose Phone Contacts, Ringvault Contacts or Enter a Number. You can also long-press a contact and choose Block Number. To unblock, swipe a number in the Blocked Numbers list.

Ringvault contacts vs. phone contacts

Ringvault contacts are private to the app. Phone contacts are the contacts in your iPhone's Contacts app. Long-press a contact to copy or move it between the two.

Photos, birthdays and addresses

Tap a contact to edit it. You can add a photo, a birthday (with or without a year) and one address. They are copied when you copy or move a contact between Ringvault and your phone contacts, and they are included in backups and shared contacts. Photos in a backup file are small copies, so they fit in a spreadsheet.

Backing up and restoring

Go to Settings › Data & Backup. Backups are CSV files saved on your iPhone (Files app › On My iPhone › Ringvault). To restore, choose Import from CSV File, or open Saved Backups and import from there. Importing adds contacts; it doesn't remove or merge existing ones.

App Lock

Turn on App Lock in Settings to require Face ID or Touch ID. If biometrics aren't available, you can unlock with your device passcode.

Contacts permission

Ringvault works without access to your phone contacts. To use phone contacts later, open the Settings app › Ringvault › Contacts.

Privacy

Ringvault keeps everything on your iPhone. Read the Privacy Policy.
