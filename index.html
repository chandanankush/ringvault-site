Ringvault

Private contacts. Calls you control.

Ringvault is an iPhone app for keeping contacts separate from your main address book, with caller ID and call blocking.

Your data stays on your iPhone. No account, no servers, no ads, no tracking.

What it does

  • Keep private contacts in Ringvault, separate from your phone contacts
  • See Ringvault contacts' names on incoming calls
  • Block numbers, and label contacts you mark as spam
  • Copy or move contacts between Ringvault and your phone contacts
  • Back up to CSV files, and lock the app with Face ID
